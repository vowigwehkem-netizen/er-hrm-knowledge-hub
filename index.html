🇳🇬 Nigerian Employment Relations & Labour Law

Core regulatory framework governing employers, employees, and trade disputes in Nigeria.

Labour Act (Cap L1 LFN 2004)

The principal legislation regulating basic terms of employment, wages, contracts, working hours, and employment standards in Nigeria.

Key Rule: Written contract required within 3 months.

Trade Disputes Act (TDA)

Provides mechanisms for resolving trade disputes through conciliation, arbitration (IAP), and the National Industrial Court (NICN).

Key Forum: National Industrial Court of Nigeria.

Employees' Compensation Act 2010

Establishes guaranteed compensation for employees or dependents in cases of workplace injury, disease, or death.

Fund: Managed by NSITF.

Pension Reform Act 2014

Mandates the Contributory Pension Scheme (CPS) for employers with 15 or more employees.

Contribution: Min 8% (Employee) & 10% (Employer).
